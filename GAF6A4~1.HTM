Put the missing orders back into Firebase

This runs the opposite way to the Sheet repair tool. It finds orders the Google Sheet has and Firebase does not — the ones typed before Firebase had finished signing in, which were silently discarded — and writes them back using the Sheet's date, customer and amounts.
The Sheet is the correct copy and is never touched here. Order ids encode the millisecond they were created, and on every order tested the Sheet's date matched that moment while Firebase's did not. So the Sheet wins on every field.

Orders you deliberately deleted are left alone — this will not resurrect them.
