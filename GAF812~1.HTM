Sheet repair v2 — bring the Sheet back in line with Firebase

Firebase is the record. This finds every order the Google Sheet is missing or has wrong, pushes it in, and removes rows for orders that were deleted in the app. Day totals rebuild themselves as it goes.
This writes to the Google Sheet. It does not touch Firebase at all — Firebase is only read. Nothing is written until you press Apply, and the dry run shows you every single change first.
Start with Safe. Only choose Everything once you have checked a few of the date differences against your own records.
