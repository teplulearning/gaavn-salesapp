Gaavn Fresh — payment backfill

order-level payments → the Payments ledger · v1 · nothing is written until step 3
What this does. June's collections were recorded on the order rows (Payment Amt / settled), not as entries in the Payments ledger. That is why Payments In shows nothing before July, and why a customer's dues cannot be rebuilt from their payment history. This finds every order-level payment that has no matching ledger entry and creates one, so every rupee received lives in one place.

The risk it guards against. Some payments exist in BOTH places already — recorded on the order and entered in the ledger. Creating another entry would count that money twice. Step 2 finds those and excludes them, and shows you the near-misses so you can judge.
