Gaavn Fresh — Firebase sign-in & roles

project gaavnfresh-sales · setup v3 · usernames on a non-routable domain, signed in once per phone
Order matters. The locked-down rules decide what someone may do by reading their users/<uid> document. Those documents have to exist first. So: enable Email/Password → create the five accounts → sign in here and assign each a role → then publish the locked rules → come back and press “Re-test”.

1 · Sign in

@gaavnfresh.invalid

Type just the username — sales1, delivery1, owner. The @gaavnfresh.invalid part is added for you and nobody has to remember it.
.invalid is reserved by internet standard — it can never be registered by anyone and mail to it can never be delivered, so no password-reset link can ever reach an outsider. Password changes are made by you in the Firebase console.

This phone stays signed in. Once you sign a phone in it remains signed in through app restarts and phone restarts, indefinitely, until someone presses Sign out. So you do this once per phone when you hand it over, and your team never types a password again.

Accounts are created by you in the Firebase console — this page never creates a user or changes a password.

2 · Give each person a role

Paste each person's UID from the console (Authentication → Users → User UID column), or sign in as them here and press "Use my own UID".

Create these four accounts in the console, with the full address including @gaavnfresh.invalid:
owner@gaavnfresh.invalid   sales1@gaavnfresh.invalid   sales2@gaavnfresh.invalid   delivery1@gaavnfresh.invalid
Your team only ever types the part before the @.

3 · Test what you can actually do

Run this before publishing the locked rules, and again straight after. Before: everything should pass because the rules are still open. After: the results should match the role you hold.
